The previous Plexus website supports the group's Halifax and Nova Scotia positioning, contact details, development and investment mandate, and interest in residential, commercial, industrial, storage, senior living, strategic land, forestry and renewable energy integration. The redesign describes these as areas of interest and does not claim that every category is an active approved project.

Lifestyle Enclave is described as 102 homes, with a residence mix of 48 one-bedroom plus den and 54 two-bedroom plus den residences from the current first-party project site. The Mineville proposal is described as 216 planned homes, 73 acres and 54 fourplex lots; density, zoning, lot size, home dimension and amenity values come from the legacy concept plan and are labelled as concept. Cornwallis Park is described as 240 acres, and the legacy phrase `zoning under review` has been replaced with a requirement to confirm parcel-specific zoning, because the archived planning strategy is already in effect. Pipeline names describe future opportunities without invented delivery dates. Renderings and concept plans are labelled throughout.

## Asset record

`asset-sources.json` records each source-backed production asset. Original filenames can differ from optimized delivery filenames. `lifestyle-arrival.webp`, `lifestyle-front.webp`, `lifestyle-lobby.webp` and `lifestyle-drone.webp` remain project-specific. `mineville.webp`, `cornwallis.webp` and `community.webp` are labelled as concepts rather than finished places. `wilmot-concept.webp` and `lucasville-concept.webp` come from the legacy commercial page; the Wilmot file has its source title band, which contains a location typo, cropped out, and both are published only inside the labelled opportunity register.

The original Plexus logo is shown inside a circular presentation without changing its artwork. Fonts are self-hosted. License notes are in `licenses/`. All interaction and animation use local CSS and JavaScript, with no external tracker or animation service.

## Implementation limits

Thirteen static pages are generated from `generate_site.py`, `sector_data.py` and `projects.json`. The enquiry form opens an email draft and does not claim server-side delivery. Project status, availability and approval details must be confirmed with Plexus. A single `LAUNCH` constant controls search indexing, canonical URLs, sharing URLs and `robots.txt` together; the review deployment keeps it `False`.
