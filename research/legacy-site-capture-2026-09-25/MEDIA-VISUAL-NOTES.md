# Legacy media visual notes

This review covers all 43 deduplicated image sources and the homepage video. Exact URLs, hashes, dimensions, responsive variants, pages, and legacy alt text are in `MEDIA-INVENTORY.md` and `metadata/media-inventory.json`.

## Overall media character

- 29 images are hosted in the site's Zyro asset library
- 13 images come from Unsplash
- 1 poster comes from Pexels
- 1 full homepage film comes from Pexels
- The collection mixes project concepts, generated marketing graphics, logos, maps, generic residential imagery, generic industrial imagery, and community stock photography
- Many images are renderings or AI-assisted concepts, not completed-project photography
- Several images have no alt text or materially incorrect alt text
- The source archive preserves all images but does not establish ownership or commercial rights for every Zyro-hosted asset

## Homepage video

The archived film is a 20.83-second silent aerial sequence over downtown Halifax and the harbour at golden hour. It is used full-screen behind the homepage content. The old site provides no title, creator, caption, or Pexels source-page link.

## Media that should not be treated as project proof

- Generic apartment building stock
- Tropical resort stock
- Generic industrial facility stock
- Detached-home subdivision stock
- AI-generated economic and sustainability graphics
- Project logos without supporting project pages
- Unlabelled renderings
- Medical-retail rendering tenant labels
- Community photos without dates, captions, or event details
- Static map with no legend
