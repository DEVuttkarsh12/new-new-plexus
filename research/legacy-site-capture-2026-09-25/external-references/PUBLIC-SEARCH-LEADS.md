# Public search and verification leads

These external sources were consulted only to identify contradictions or official planning context. They are not a substitute for client confirmation, title documents, permits, or current project records.

## Official Cornwallis Park planning

Archived locally:

- `Cornwallis_Park_Secondary_Planning_Strategy_2026-06-08.pdf`
- `Cornwallis_Park_Secondary_Planning_Strategy_2026-06-08.txt`

Source:

- https://annapoliscounty.ca/images/Planning__Inspection_Services/Planning_Documents/CORNWALLIS_PARK_SPS_EFFECTIVE_June_8_2026.pdf

Key relevance:

- Effective March 27, 2025
- Amended June 8, 2026
- Establishes neighbourhood, commercial, mixed-use, industrial, public-use, and parkland policy for Cornwallis Park
- Explicitly discusses the Normandy Road area
- Supports the general need for housing, community amenities, and commercial/employment growth
- Does not prove Plexus ownership or validate the 240-acre concept

This makes the legacy site's simple statement that zoning is “under review” potentially stale. Parcel-specific zoning still needs direct verification.
